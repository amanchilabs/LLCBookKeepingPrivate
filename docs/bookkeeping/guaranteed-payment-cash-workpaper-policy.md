# Guaranteed payment cash workpaper policy

The guaranteed-payment cash CSV contains actual payment and reversal events
whose event dates fall within the selected inclusive reporting period. Each row
includes the member, existing services or use-of-capital classification, cash
account, description, payment ID, transaction ID, and reversal reason.

The signed workpaper amount uses the cash-basis workpaper direction: actual
payments are positive and returned funds are negative. The separate cash
movement column uses the journal cash direction: payments are negative and
returns are positive. Period totals are shown separately for services and use
of capital, plus their combined totals, so the cash side can reconcile to
journal activity.

Contractual installments and unpaid obligations are not cash events and are
excluded. The workpaper does not determine tax classification, deductibility,
or capitalization and is not a tax form.
