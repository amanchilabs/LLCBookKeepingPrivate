# Loan cash workpaper policy

The Loan Cash Activity CSV contains only funded advances and actual repayments
whose dates fall in the selected inclusive reporting period. It traces each
event to its loan agreement, activity, transaction, journal entry, lender, and
cash account.

Cash movement is positive for an advance into the business and negative for a
repayment from the business. Principal movement is positive for funded
principal and negative for principal repaid. Actual interest paid is shown as
a separate positive amount. Thus cash movement reconciles as principal
movement less interest paid. Period totals sum each column independently.

Loan commitments are not advances, loan proceeds are not income, principal
repayments are not expenses, and accrued-interest estimates are excluded from
this cash activity export. The workpaper does not select interest rates or
determine deductibility and is not a tax form.
