# Proposed Screenshot Inventory

Review scope: every PNG in [`screenshots/`](screenshots/) was inspected. The folder contains **31 image files** describing **20 distinct screen types**. One file is a five-screen onboarding contact sheet; repeated concepts are marked as alternatives below. These are design proposals, not evidence that a screen or interaction is implemented.

“Current source” refers to the checked-in native React Native implementation at the design-review base commit. It does not claim simulator or device verification.

## Inventory reconciliation

## Distinct visual elements and interaction cues

The following cues distinguish the variants and indicate what each picture proposes. They are visual/content observations, not verified behavior.

1. **LLC Bookkeeping Onboarding Flow.png** — one contact sheet with five phone frames and progress treatment; proposes forward/back setup steps and a review/create endpoint.
2. **Private LLC Bookkeeping Onboarding.png** — warm welcome card, privacy/local-use benefit statements and page dots; proposes Get started and skip-intro actions.
3. **LLC Setup Onboarding Screen.png** — compact legal/business facts with a tax-classification selector; selector is the unsupported unique element.
4. **LLC Members and Ownership Setup.png** — ownership template action, editable member rows and live percentage total; template behavior is proposed.
5. **First Account Setup Onboarding Screen.png** — account-kind choice and opening-balance fields inside the wizard; add-another/skip actions are proposed.
6. **LLC Onboarding Review Screen.png** — grouped business/member/account summary cards with per-section edit affordances before create.
7. **LLC Workspace Ready Screen.png** — celebratory landscape/illustration with dashboard and tour calls to action; tour is not implemented.
8. **LLC Financial Dashboard Overview.png** — populated KPI cards, ownership donut, recent activity and quick-action shortcuts; opening-balance and reversal labels need correction.
9. **LLC Finance Dashboard Overview.png** — sparse/zero-activity month chart, fiscal-period control and “Record Journal” shortcut; shortcut is unsupported.
10. **LLC Transactions Dashboard (1).png** — search/filter count, multi-filter chips, date-grouped rows and group subtotals; richer history variant.
11. **LLC Transactions Dashboard.png** — three summary metrics, simple type chips and shorter list; less discoverable search/filter treatment.
12. **LLC Bookkeeping Add Transaction Screen.png** — transaction type selector and straightforward form, then optional member assignment; remove assignment.
13. **LLCBookKeeping Transaction Entry Screen.png** — same form concept with default-on member split controls and ownership-derived percentages; reject that allocation interaction.
14. **Transaction Details and Member Allocation.png** — full metadata, status badge and vertical audit timeline with prominent action row; remove member allocation/edit/delete.
15. **Transaction Details and Member Allocations.png** — more compact header and tabular split summary, with duplicate/edit/reverse/delete action row; retain only useful hierarchy/audit cues.
16. **LLC Members Ownership Dashboard.png** — ownership donut, contribution totals, and member rows combining interest with contribution amounts; keep the measures distinct.
17. **LLC Members & Ownership Dashboard.png** — four summary cards, donut, managing-role label and row overflow menus; role controls are unsupported.
18. **LLC Members Dashboard UI.png** — dense contribution table, member contribution totals and current cash card plus role/permission area; current cash is business-level, not member-level.
19. **Member Capital Ledger Dashboard.png** — member sub-tabs, balance tiles and dated capital ledger; its mixed debt/payment rows are misleading.
20. **Modern Guaranteed Payments Dashboard.png** — paid-versus-outstanding progress, recurring obligation card and payment activity; strong obligation/payment separation cue.
21. **Member Loan Overview Dashboard.png** — borrower/lender summary tiles, principal/repayment table, interest calculator and export controls; commitment/funding distinction is missing.
22. **Accounts & Reconciliation Dashboard.png** — Accounts/Reconcile/Activity sub-tabs, account-status badges, recent statement rows and reconciliation CTA; no bank-feed implementation.
23. **Northstar Ventures Financial Reports.png** — standard report list and quick KPI cards with export/schedule buttons; schedule action is unsupported.
24. **LLC Financial Reports Dashboard.png** — report-type cards including Tax Ready Export plus contribution/transaction quick links; reject tax-ready label.
25. **LLC Reports & Analytics Dashboard.png** — period selectors, monthly chart, report tiles and email/share controls; chart/email behavior is not present in current report UI.
26. **Profit & Loss Statement Dashboard.png** — report header/date span, category detail, net result and margin visual; cash-basis/date label must be explicit.
27. **LLC Balance Sheet Report Detail.png** — Report/Summary/Compare/Export tabs, final-status badge and member equity split; compare/final and ownership-derived capital are unsupported.
28. **LLCBookKeeping More Settings Screen.png** — broad grouped settings lists with data management, roles, preference and notifications rows; several actions are unsupported/destructive.
29. **LLC Bookkeeping Settings Screen.png** — LLC card and grouped Company/Data/App Settings/Help sections in a compact list; roles, lock and notification claims are not current.
30. **Secure & Private LLC Bookkeeping Settings.png** — security toggles, cloud-backup switch, analytics toggle and delete-data row; conflicts with approved privacy and security state.
31. **Private Security Settings for LLC Bookkeeping.png** — trust/authorized-member privacy banner, app-lock controls and iCloud restore section; multi-user and cloud implications are disallowed.

- Repeated visual families: onboarding welcome/setup variants; two dashboard layouts; two transaction-list layouts; two transaction-entry forms; two transaction-detail layouts; three member-list layouts; three reports landing layouts; two More settings lists; and two security/privacy pages.
- Unique concepts with no alternative image: setup review and ready states; member capital ledger; guaranteed-payment summary; member loan summary; accounts/reconciliation; P&L detail; Balance Sheet detail.

- **31** PNG files inspected; **20** unique screen types identified.
- **20** canonical individual screen images are selected in the companion specifications. The onboarding contact sheet is a reference composite, not an additional screen type. The other 10 files are alternative concepts for already counted screens.
- All 31 are design mockups. None is a screenshot of the current running app, and none proves that its pictured feature exists.
- Empty, validation, destructive-confirmation, loading, error, and offline/locked states are largely absent. Their requirements are listed in `screen-specifications.md` and `acceptance-criteria.md`.
